1. Complete com o verbo correto

A) She is working now.
B) They are playing at the moment.
C) He is studying English.
D) I am drinking coffee.

2. Negativa

A) She is working = She is not (isn’t) working
B) He is studying = He is not (isn’t) studying
C) They are playing = They are not (aren’t) playing

3. Pergunta

A) You are working = Are you working?
B) She is studying = Is she studying?
C) They are playing = Are they playing?

4. Corrija os erros

A) I working now = I am working now
B) She is study = She is studying
C) I am knowing the answer = I know the answer

5. Traduza

A) I am studying now =  Eu estou estudando agora  
B) We are developing a system = Nós estamos desenvolvendo um sistema
C) She is working at the moment = Ela está trabalhando no momento
