----- SIMPLE PRESENT ------
 
1. I run every morning.
2. She swims very well.
3. They sit near the window.
4. The car stops at the signal.
5. We plan our trips carefully.
6. He travels every month.
7. Maria dances at school parties.
8. My friend writes in a notebook.
9. My mother makes delicious cakes.
10. My father drives to work.
11. The teacher comes early.
12. I take the bus to school.
13. They move to a new house every year.
14. The dog lies on the sofa.
15. I get up at 6 o’clock.
16. My mother shops on Saturdays.
17. The class begins at 8 a.m.
18. He forgets his keys sometimes.
19. We use computers at school.
20. She lives near the beach.
